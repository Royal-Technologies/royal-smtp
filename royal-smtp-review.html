📧 Royal SMTP

Reliable WordPress Email Delivery

Why Choose Royal SMTP? WordPress's default mail function often fails to deliver emails reliably. Royal SMTP solves this by connecting your site to trusted SMTP servers like Gmail, Outlook, Amazon SES, SendGrid, Mailgun, and more—ensuring your emails always reach the inbox.

🚀 Powerful Features at Your Fingertips

Royal SMTP comes with everything you need to ensure reliable email delivery:

📮 Multiple SMTP Providers: Support for Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, Amazon SES, SendGrid, Mailgun, SMTP.com, Brevo, Postmark, SparkPost, and custom SMTP servers.
✅ One-Click Test Email: Instantly verify your SMTP configuration works correctly with our built-in email tester.
🔌 Connection Test: Test SMTP server connectivity before sending any emails to ensure everything is set up correctly.
📋 Email Logs: Keep track of every email sent from your site with detailed logging, including recipient, subject, and delivery status.
🔐 Secure Authentication: Full support for TLS/SSL encryption, OAuth 2.0 for Gmail/Outlook, and secure credential storage.
🎨 Beautiful Admin Interface: Modern, intuitive dashboard with SweetAlert2 notifications and responsive design.
📊 DataTables Integration: Powerful sorting, filtering, and pagination for email logs with export capabilities.
🌐 Multilingual Ready: Full translation support with Bengali (বাংলা) included out of the box.
